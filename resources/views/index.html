How it works

Register in four steps

No queues at the registry desk and no paper forms.

  1. Step 01

    Sign in

    Log in with your MUBS email. New students create an account with their registration number.

  2. Step 02

    Browse courses

    See every course open this semester with its code, credits, and lecturer.

  3. Step 03

    Register

    One click registers you. The system refuses a second registration for the same course.

  4. Step 04

    Get approved

    Your lecturer confirms the registration and you get a notification.

Why use the system

Stop carrying paper forms between the registry and the faculty

Course registration should not depend on finding the registry open. The student picks the courses, the lecturer sees who signed up, and the administrator keeps one record that everyone reads from.

Follow your own registration from submitted to approved
Students only ever see their own records
Duplicate registrations are rejected by the database
Every change is kept with who did it and when

One student record

Registration number, programme, year, and contacts in one place.

Courses with lecturers

Every course shows its code, credits, and the lecturer who teaches it.

Lecturers see their classes

A lecturer opens their dashboard and sees exactly who registered for their courses.

Roles decide access

Administrator, lecturer, and student roles control who can do what.
